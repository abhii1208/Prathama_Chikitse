## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an authoritative, life-critical emergency companion experience that marries the urgency of clinical trauma triage with the elevated poise of premium medical publishing. Built primarily for high-stress, time-sensitive mobile interactions, it bridges urgent physical action with quiet cognitive reassurance.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Decisive & Calm:** Eliminates visual noise under panic conditions while instilling steady confidence through structured typographic cadence.
- **Surgical Precision:** Every component adheres to strict hierarchy, high contrast boundaries, and unambiguous touch ergonomics.
- **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** Combines the gravitas of a classic medical compendium with the responsiveness of modern digital triage.

### Aesthetic Direction: Modern Clinical-Editorial
The aesthetic merges high-contrast editorial authority with tactile digital ergonomics:
- **Rich Tonal Purple Atmosphere:** Replaces sterile, anxiety-inducing hospital whites with deep, reassuring royal indigo and layered soft purples to focus visual attention and reduce optical fatigue.
- **High-Alert Emergency Interventions:** High-consequence actions—such as emergency dialing (`CALL 112`) and critical resuscitation timers—cut through the calm indigo palette using intense, unapologetic emergency red.
- **Thumb-Driven Ergonomics:** Built around a 390×844 mobile viewport baseline, placing primary life-support guides, offline badges, and rapid dial triggers within the natural primary thumb sweep zone (48px+ minimum target boundaries).

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate distinction between calm clinical instruction and acute life-safety interventions.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#10069F` - Electric Royal Purple):** The anchor for brand identity, critical section headers, primary interactive surfaces, and top-tier navigation bars.
- **Secondary (`#2515D4` - Bright Purple):** Utilized for active states, interactive focus rings, key pathway triggers, and progress tracking markers.
- **Deep Navy Purple (`#08005F`):** Provides deep grounding surfaces, night-mode readiness, header containers, and high-contrast typographic accents.
- **Soft Purple (`#E9E7FF`) & Very Light Purple (`#F5F4FF`):** Tonal surface tints that host structured instructional cards, step-by-step triage groups, and nested metadata without introducing harsh contrast lines.
- **Emergency Red (`#D92D20`):** Reserved exclusively for life-threatening scenarios: direct EMS dispatch triggers (`CALL 112`), cardiac arrest indicators, and critical counter-warnings. Never diluted or used decoratively.
- **Warning Amber (`#E6A000`):** Indicates precautions, contraindications, and pre-triage escalations.
- **Success Green (`#18864B`):** Confirms stable vitals, completed first-aid sequences, and active offline synchronization.
- **Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-elevation floating cards, modal surfaces, and primary canvas backgrounds.
- **Near Black (`#111116`) & Body Gray (`#5F606B`):** Text hierarchy ensuring WCAG AAA legibility for high-stress reading under harsh sunlight or dark environments.
- **Subtle Border (`#DDDCE6`):** Structural division for data cards and input perimeters.

### Application Rules
1. **The Emergency Red Isolation Rule:** No UI element may share `#D92D20` unless it initiates an immediate contact dispatch, logs an irreversible emergency condition, or highlights fatal contraindications.
2. **Surface Layering:** Screens default to `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F4FF` base canvasing, placing procedures on structured `#FFFFFF` cards with `#DDDCE6` boundary definitions and `#E9E7FF` informational headers.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an intentional friction between reflective medical authority and rapid-fire comprehension.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Playfair Display:** Carries clinical heritage, chapter titles, emergency condition headings (e.g., *Anaphylaxis*, *Severe Hemorrhage*), and primary guide introductions. It conveys institutional credibility and historical authority.
- **Inter:** The operational engine. Applied to every actionable step, dosage chart, triage bullet, navigation tab, and button. Its clean geometric design ensures immediate legibility even under high glare or hand tremors.

### Scaling & Legibility Directives
- **Step-by-Step Triage Instructions:** Must use `body-xl` (18px) or `body-lg` (16px) with an increased line height (`lineHeight: 28px` / `24px`) to ensure reading clarity while performing chest compressions or applying dressings.
- **Emergency Callouts:** `emergency-callout` is rendered in uppercase with tight spacing for quick identification on dispatch banners and quick-dial keys.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is engineered around a fluid, mobile-first column model optimized for single-handed operation on a 390×844 base canvas.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Viewport Anchor:** Standardized for the 390×844 profile with critical action anchors placed in the bottom 40% thumb zone.
- **Grid Architecture:** 4-column fluid layout on mobile screens with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters and `1rem` (16px) canvas side margins. Expands to an 8-column layout on tablets (600px+) and 12 columns on desktop viewports.
- **The 48px Ergonomic Safe Boundary:** No interactive element (button, step confirmation checkbox, emergency dialer, tab item) may measure under 48×48px in touchable surface area.

### Spacing Rhythm
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-spacing between tags, step badges, and status pills.
- `space-sm` (8px): Internal gaps between list icons and triage instructions.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard padding inside cards, between distinct protocol steps, and input grouping.
- `space-lg` (24px): Structural margins separating thematic sections (e.g., Symptoms vs. Immediate Actions).
- `space-xl` (32px): Separation before persistent bottom docks and global emergency anchors.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a layered, tonal surface-stacking approach combined with colored ambient shadows.

### Elevation Architecture
1. **Canvas Base (`Level 0`):** Soft foundation in `#F5F4FF` or pure `#FFFFFF`. Neutral, quiet, and matte.
2. **Card Surfaces (`Level 1`):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` containers bounded by a 1px solid stroke of `#DDDCE6`. Layered above the background with a soft, tinted drop shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(16, 6, 159, 0.06), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(17, 17, 22, 0.04);`
3. **Interactive Overlays & Quick-Action Docks (`Level 2`):** Floating navigation bars, active dosage calculation trays, and offline notification badges:
   - `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(16, 6, 159, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(17, 17, 22, 0.06);`
4. **Emergency Bottom Sheet & Urgent Alerts (`Level 3`):** High-priority triage cards and the persistent `CALL 112` bottom tray:
   - `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -8px rgba(8, 0, 95, 0.22), 0 4px 12px rgba(217, 45, 32, 0.15);`

### Border Philosophy
Elevations avoid murky gray drop shadows. Depth is reinforced via subtle 1px inner or perimeter boundaries using `#DDDCE6` or light brand tint `#E9E7FF`, preserving structural clarity under stress.

## Shapes

The design system uses a balanced geometric shape language (`roundedness: 2`), projecting clinical order softened by approachable touch targets.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Standard Corners (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to form fields, badge containers, and nested step indicators.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / 16px):** Standard curvature for instructional cards, triage summaries, and floating symptom trays.
- **Prominent Surfaces & Bottom Sheets (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / 24px):** Applied to modal sheets, triage header containers, and primary category blocks.
- **Pills & Circular Badges (`9999px`):** Used for step numbers, status pills (e.g., *Offline Database Ready*), and the rapid `CALL 112` floating pill button.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Emergency Action Button (EMS Direct):** Solid `#D92D20` background, pure `#FFFFFF` bold uppercase typography (`emergency-callout`), 56px height, rounded pill shape (`9999px`). Includes a phone receiver icon on the left. Active state shifts to dark crimson (`#B42318`) with haptic feedback.
- **Primary Protocol Button:** Deep electric royal purple `#10069F` fill, `#FFFFFF` text (`label-lg`), 52px height, 16px radius (`rounded-lg`). For progressing to the next resuscitation step or beginning a guided CPR metronome.
- **Secondary / Review Button:** `#E9E7FF` surface with `#10069F` text, zero border, 48px height. Provides safe, non-urgent actions.

### Cards & Triage Step Modules
- **Step Card:** Clean white background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px `#DDDCE6` boundary, 16px radius. Displays a circular badge on the left (32×32px, filled with `#E9E7FF`, text `#10069F` in `label-md`) displaying the step number. Primary instructions sit right-aligned in `body-xl` (`#111116`).
- **Contraindication Card (Warning):** Light amber background (`#FFFBEB`), amber left accent border (4px solid `#E6A000`), containing explicit warnings of what *not* to do.

### Lists & Action Checklists
- **Clinical Checklist Item:** 56px minimum height container. Left-aligned custom checkbox (24×24px, 6px radius, border 2px `#DDDCE6`; checked state is solid `#18864B` with white checkmark). Accompanied by high-contrast `body-lg` text.

### Inputs & Dosage Calculators
- **Numerical Inputs:** 52px height, `#FFFFFF` fill, 2px border `#DDDCE6`. Focus state highlights to `#2515D4` with a 3px outer glow in `#E9E7FF`. Font size remains locked at 16px to avoid mobile browser auto-zooming.

### Chips & Badges
- **Offline Indicator Badge:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`), 28px height, `#E9E7FF` fill, `#10069F` text, accompanied by a 6×6px `#18864B` solid green status dot denoting 100% locally cached medical guides.
- **Triage Level Tag:** High-contrast, uppercase text (`label-sm`).
  - *Immediate (P1):* `#D92D20` text on `#FEE4E2` background.
  - *Delayed (P2):* `#B54708` text on `#FEF0C7` background.
  - *Minimal (P3):* `#18864B` text on `#D1FADF` background.

### Specialized Medical Additions
- **CPR Metronome Bar:** Bottom-anchored or card-level pulse component beating visually at 100–120 BPM. Cycles between `#10069F` and `#2515D4` with an audible/haptic pulse toggle.
- **Floating Emergency Dock:** Persistent bottom bar hovering over content with a high-contrast `CALL 112` trigger on the right side and the current procedure step on the left.
